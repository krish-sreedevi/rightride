1. What Right Ride is

Right Ride is a free information service. It helps you compare new cars on sale in India, find the variant that has the features you want, estimate the on-road price in your state and find showrooms near you. We are not a car dealer, broker, lender or insurer. We don't sell cars, take bookings or payments, or act for any carmaker or dealer.

2. Prices and specifications

  • Where it comes from. Ex-showroom prices, variants and features are collected automatically from each manufacturer's official website and refreshed every week. If a maker doesn't publish something, we may use a reputable Indian car website instead. See "Data sources & coverage" in the site footer for details.
  • On-road prices are estimates. We add our estimates of road tax, registration, insurance, FASTag and TCS for the state you choose. The real price depends on your city, dealer, insurer, offers, accessories and the date you buy. Always confirm the final price with your dealer before booking.
  • Things change. Carmakers change prices, variants and features without notice, and automated collection can make mistakes. Information may be out of date, incomplete or wrong until our next refresh. If you spot an error, please tell us at info@rightride.in.
  • Mileage and range are the maker's claimed or certified figures. Real-world numbers are usually lower.
  • Safety ratings come from Bharat NCAP, Global NCAP or ASEAN NCAP. They apply to the version that was tested, which may differ from the one on sale now.

3. Scores and recommendations

Expert scores summarise published reviews. Match scores, "Best for you" picks, the Great / Good / Weak ratings on the compare page, space and resale estimates, and "Your Right Variant" suggestions are worked out by Right Ride from those reviews, maker data and your answers. They are opinions to help you shortlist, not financial, legal or professional advice, and not a promise about any car. Test-drive the car and do your own checks before you buy.
